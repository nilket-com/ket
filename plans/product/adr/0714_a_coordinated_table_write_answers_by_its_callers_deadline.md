# ADR 0714: a coordinated table write answers by its caller's deadline

Status: proposed, 2026-11-11 (Claude, Codex). Direction accepted with an ownership hold (Codex 01a1c958, `reviews/pd-deadline/CODEX-DESIGN-R1.txt`); design cleared with constraints (Codex 01a1c95c, `reviews/pd-deadline/CODEX-DESIGN-CLEAR.txt`). Source review pending. Implementation lands with this plan. Review record: `reviews/pd-deadline/` (`CENSUS.md`, `DESIGN-0714.md`, `ADDENDUM-1.md`, `impl/`). This ADR is the as-built consolidation of those; where they differ, this text and the later ruling govern.

## Why
- **The table write of a ParentDecisions node ignored its caller's deadline.** `deadline_remaining_millis` was dropped at the mode branch of `table_write`; the coordinator never read it. ADR 0709 and ADR 0710 both recorded this as not established.
- **So one server attempt had no bound its caller could set.** A follower-coordinated one-child write could take about 20 s of per-RPC deadlines plus unbounded local waits; a leader-coordinated one has no derivable bound. The client has no per-attempt timeout, so the caller's deadline bounded nothing.
- **Simply dropping the work at the deadline is not acceptable after a Reserve.** A Reserved parent has no time expiry and no background finisher: an abandoned one stays Reserved, holding a live slot, until a retry of the same identity or a lineage close.
- ParentDecisions is opt-in and not shipped. Nothing here is a statement about the shipped mode.

## Decision
**In ParentDecisions mode, the coordination of a table write that names a deadline answers by that deadline: WRITE_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED, INDETERMINATE, stop reason DEADLINE. The deadline alone does not abandon a coordination that already owns or resumes a reservation: the handler that answered goes on polling that same coordination to its end.** The budget covers the coordinator only. What the handler does with an ordinary result after the coordinator returns is outside it (see the limits).

1. **One budget.** `caller_deadline_from_remaining_millis` converts the wire value once, at the coordinator's entry (`coordinate_table_write`). It is one absolute instant, never renewed per phase, per child or per RPC. The lookups are inside it. Absent (zero) means no deadline, and the path is as before.
2. **The answer at the deadline is always INDETERMINATE.** Never KNOWN_NOT_COMMITTED and never an abort: this attempt, or an earlier one of the same identity, may have reserved. Nothing durable records it. It is not in `retryable_mutation_status`, so the last-attempt rule neither rewrites nor records it. The client does not retry WRITE_DEADLINE_EXCEEDED; that is unchanged.
3. **One gate per attempt: Open, Owned, Expired** (`network/data_server/write_deadline.rs`, `AttemptGate`).
   - **The attempt claims it at its two boundary points only:** immediately before the Reserve proposal is first polled, and immediately before a Reserved record found by the lookup is resumed (children, then Finalize). A claim reads the clock and takes the gate with nothing awaited between the two. If the clock is past the deadline the claim refuses, even if no timer has run yet. Expired never becomes Owned.
   - **The deadline expires it** when its timer fires. If the gate was still Open, the attempt is before its boundary: its future is dropped, having submitted no Reserve, child or Finalize, and it could never claim afterwards. (Before a claim the attempt reads, and `merge_commit_hlc` advances the local clock; nothing of the write is submitted.) If the gate is Owned, the deadline's answer is given and the future is kept.
   - **Owned means** "a submission may start, or an existing reservation is being resumed". It does not mean a Reserve committed. From Owned on, the coordination's future is never dropped for the deadline.
   - **The claim is a value** (`OwnedAttempt`) that only a successful claim constructs; the Reserve and Finalize proposals of a write take it.
   - **A tie goes to the result.** A coordination result that is ready when the handler is polled is the answer, even if the timer is also due. A deadline already past at entry is answered without polling the coordination at all.
   - After a deadline answer, the coordination's own result is discarded. It is not a second answer.
4. **Ownership: the remainder never leaves the stream's task.** No task is spawned and no background owner is added.
   - The router's `handle_stream` hands a table write's stream to the data ring's stream entry, after its ingress check, as it already does for bulk writes and queries. The data server's mode check for the served set comes first there, unchanged.
   - `serve_table_write_stream` (`network/data_server/write_replication.rs`) polls the write. If the write ends with its answer, that is the one response, as before. If the write gives its answer early, the response is written and the stream finished while the same call keeps polling the same write; the two are joined, and neither a failed response write nor a failed finish cancels the write. The call returns only when the write has ended.
   - Therefore the stream admission (the per-connection stream slot and the global stream-task permit), the two lifecycle guards and the tenant request admission are all held until the coordination ends. An expired request keeps its stream slot, so many short-deadline calls meet the existing stream limits; they cannot create coordinators beyond today's admission capacity.
   - The tenant authorization, the request admission, the payload decoding and the last-attempt rule are one function (`serve_table_write`), shared by the stream entry and the frame entry. The early answer passes the last-attempt rule too.
   - An entry with no stream (a frame handed straight to `handle_data_frame`) cannot answer early. It never drops an Owned coordination either: its deadline answer is returned when the coordination has ended.
5. **Unchanged:** the KV write (no deadline on its wire; its gate never refuses), the table delete, Today mode, the path with no deadline, every wire format, the records and the state machine.

## The guarantee, and its limits
- **Is:** in ParentDecisions mode, the coordinator of a table write that names a deadline has its answer by that deadline: either its own result, or the deadline answer, which is handed to the stream write then. And the deadline alone does not abandon a coordination that already owns or resumes a reservation.
- **It is a cooperative response-readiness bound plus scheduling slack, not a real-time guarantee.** The timer and the coordination are polled by one task; a synchronous stretch of a poll, or a busy runtime, delays the answer.
- **Is not:**
  - the whole caller-deadline obligation. **The timer wraps `coordinate`, not what follows it.** When the coordinator returns an ordinary result before the deadline, `handle_table_write` passes it to `finalize_table_write_attempt`. On a last attempt with a retryable status (for example NOT_LEADER) that call records the outcome: `record_mutation_outcome` → `mutation_outcomes.upsert`, which awaits `write_lock.lock()` and does database work. No timer covers that wait, and the early-answer sender is already gone, so the wire answer can come after the deadline. This is asynchronous work after the coordinator, not scheduling slack and not a slow caller. It remains an open deadline gap; this ADR does not close it. (For the deadline answer itself that call is a no-op: the status is not retryable.)
  - guaranteed eventual Accepted. The remainder can still end INDETERMINATE with the parent Reserved (a child or decision RPC fails, quorum is short). Nor is the reserve guaranteed to exist: a submission that was started may never have applied. Resolution is then as before: a call with the same identity, or a lineage close.
  - a change to crash recovery.
  - a bound on the remainder. It takes what a write with no deadline can take.
  - an end-to-end response time. The budget starts where the wire value is converted, after the frame is read and admitted; time queued before that is not counted. For the deadline answer, "ready" means handed to the stream write; delivery to a caller that is not reading is not promised.
  - successful finalization at shutdown. The order is unchanged: Raft stops before the bounded wait for request quiescence, as it already does, so a remainder in flight then may be unable to finish. An incomplete close keeps its existing error and fail-closed semantics.
  - a statement about the shipped mode. `three_node_workflow` passing remains opt-in-mode evidence.

## Acceptance (finite)
Tests are in `node::parent_decisions_cluster_tests::write_deadline` (three real nodes; every write is one request frame on a stream into the node's published service, read for its one response frame) and `network::data_server::write_deadline::tests` (the gate alone).
1. **The RED.** `a_write_held_after_its_reserve_answers_by_its_deadline_and_is_completed_unretried`: deadline 300 ms, coordinator held after its Reserve. The response frame is received while the hold is in place; the handler has not returned and the record is Reserved. Released and never retried, the record is Accepted with the reserved claim, the row reads on every node, a call with the same identity answers COMMITTED at the recorded decision, and there is one record. On main's unchanged production code it fails: no response while held (`reviews/pd-deadline/impl/RED.txt`).
2. **Before the boundary.** Held after the lookup found the key absent: the same answer, the handler ends with nothing left to own, and no record exists then or after the release. A later call of the identity commits as a first one.
3. **The resume path.** A parent reserved by an earlier, held attempt; a second attempt with a deadline resumes it and is held before Finalize, past its claim: the deadline answer while held, then that same attempt completes the parent after release. And with the clock already past the deadline at each claim point, with no timer: the deadline answer, the Reserved record unchanged (no terminal transition), and under a fresh identity no record. That the refused attempt did no child work is not shown by that assertion; it follows from the source order, where the claim precedes `write_children`.
4. **Lifecycle.** Through the production accept loop with the node's router: after the timed-out response is received, the stream task and its request are still tracked, its stream-task permit is still taken and the lifecycle is not quiescent; after release it is quiescent, the permit is back and the record is Accepted. And a response that cannot be written: the handler has not ended well after the deadline, and after release it reports the failed write and the record is Accepted, read on every node.
5. **The gate alone.** A claim with the clock past the deadline refuses before any timer ran; Expired is never claimed and Owned is never expired; a ready result wins a tie; a deadline already past never polls the attempt; an attempt that has not claimed is dropped; one that claimed is answered at its deadline and polled to its end.
6. **Ordinary success** with a generous deadline and with none, on the stream. A table write stream from a non-node actor is refused at ingress.
7. The ADR 0709 and ADR 0710 controls, the delete's deadline control and `three_node_workflow` pass unchanged. Focused runs, source review, then one checkpoint at the tip. No stress campaign and no memory run.

## Not established
- **A bound on the wire answer of an ordinary result that needs last-attempt outcome persistence** (the gap described under the limits: `finalize_table_write_attempt` → `record_mutation_outcome` → `mutation_outcomes.upsert`, outside the budget).
- Any bound on how long a remainder works after the answer: local lock and persistence waits and a local leader's Raft waits stay unbounded.
- Any bound for KV writes.
- Resolution of an abandoned Reserved parent beyond what exists. A Reserved parent still has no time expiry and Abort still has no producer.
- Behaviour of the answer under a saturated runtime: the slack is not measured.
- A control at shutdown with a remainder in flight. None was written; the order is as read in `node.rs`, not exercised here.
- The work observation of a table write (`observe_table_write`) and its tracing span now cover the remainder too, since they wrap the same future. No metric was added for early answers.

## Registered separately, not changed here
- **DC-212 (open, serious candidate).** In ParentDecisions mode `finalize_table_write_attempt` rewrites a last-attempt NOT_LEADER, which the coordinator returns as INDETERMINATE for a Reserved parent held by another coordinator, to WRITE_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED + KNOWN_NOT_COMMITTED and records it, although that parent can still be Accepted. From source; reachability from the real client is not traced.
- **The table delete differs.** Its lookups are outside its budget, a recorded outcome is answered whatever the deadline, and at expiry after its Reserve it drops its work, leaving the parent Reserved until a retry or a lineage close. Recorded as a difference; it is not evidence for this contract and is not changed here.
