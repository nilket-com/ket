# ADR 0706: certified steps finish before a clean close

Status: proposed, 2026-11-10 (Claude, Codex). Design cleared (Codex 01a1c44c). Implementation lands with this plan. Closes DC-197 when its implementation and evidence are reviewed. Lands after ADR 0705's pair; one consolidated checkpoint at the final tip. Review record: `reviews/dc195-layout-collision/` (DC-197 entries).

## Why
- **DC-197 (availability; fails closed):** a complete close of a ParentDecisions node can record an unclean shutdown, so its next start validates as after a crash. Observed only in `certified_tier_cleanup_rewrites_and_retires_the_leaders_tiered_slabs`, at the harness's close ("the close recorded a clean shutdown"): main ab3a0628d 0/5 runs under load, ADR 0705's candidate 2/6. Attributing those two failures to the mechanism below is an inference until the control in Acceptance 1 ties them together.
- **Mechanism (source):** `certified_tier_step` (ADR 0704) and `certified_lsm_step_unpaused` (ADR 0703), `network/data_server/cleanup.rs`, take a `LifecyclePermit` in the caller's future and complete it after the step. They run in the LSM compaction service, which `NodeHandles::abort` aborts with no drain (`node.rs`, `lsm_compaction.abort()`). An abort mid-step drops the permit uncompleted, and `LifecyclePermit::drop` revokes the run's clean-shutdown record (`storage/ownership.rs`). The permit's contract says it must be kept until the work finishes, even across cancellation.
- **Census of permit holders (source):** the decision maintenance loop's steps (`certification_step`, `certified_cleanup_step`, through `under_permit`) are drained by `Node::shutdown` before Raft stops (ADR 0699 S5a, `drain_decision_maintenance`); ADR 0703's install recovery and the LSM run rename take their permit where they spawn the work. The two certified steps of the LSM compaction service are the only holders a shutdown cancels.

## Decision
**The LSM compaction service stops between ticks, like the decision maintenance loop: `Node::shutdown` signals it and drains it, bounded, while Raft still runs; only past the bound is it aborted, and that interruption keeps revoking the clean record (fail closed, as today).**

1. **A stop signal.** `LsmCompactionService` gains a sticky stop (a `watch`, as decision maintenance's `maintenance_stop`) that wakes a sleeping or paused service. It is checked at the tick admission boundary, never selected against an in-flight tick: once the service observes it, no new tick is admitted. A tick admitted before (including one that races the stop) finishes, its remaining certified substeps included, each to its own terminal return (published, refused with whatever durable intent and recovery state its operation requires, as today, or failed). Pause/resume are unchanged.
2. **Shutdown order.** `Node::shutdown` signals both stops (the LSM service's and decision maintenance's) before waiting for either, then drains both concurrently under one shared deadline: the existing 15-second bound, renamed as the bound on lifecycle services' orderly stop, so shutdown latency does not double. All of this happens before `lifecycle.begin_drain()` and `raft.shutdown()`: Raft, the committer and the transports stay live until the drain ends, so a step's proposals and waits terminate normally.
3. **Past the bound:** the service is aborted **and joined** before shutdown proceeds, so the interrupted permit has dropped and revoked the clean record; the next start is unclean and validates. No step is marked complete by a cancellation path. A panic is surfaced as a join failure, never treated as orderly completion; interrupted shutdown and the storage-close timeout stay fail closed. A drained handle is consumed (its completion represented explicitly), so `NodeHandles::shutdown` never polls it again; the direct `abort` paths stay abrupt and fail closed. Exhausting the drain bound makes `Node::shutdown` return a `TimedOut` error after the abort and join; a storage-close error takes precedence over it. The `LifecyclePermit` contract is unchanged.
4. **No detached step.** The step is not moved into a spawned task. Detached work owning its permit would be drained by the owner's close, but it would rely on Raft after Raft's shutdown and add task-lifetime complexity; the pre-Raft drain is the simpler fix. (It replaces the spawned direction accepted in 01a1c44a.)
5. **The census becomes a check:** a small architecture test lists every production site that takes a `LifecyclePermit` (`lifecycle_permit()`, `under_permit(`) and the owner that keeps it across cancellation (a drained service, or the spawned task that owns the work); a new site fails until it is classified. It is a classification guard, not a proof of cancellation safety: the behavioural controls are the evidence.

## Acceptance (finite)
1. **Orderly close waits for a held step**, for each of the two certified steps (a test hold inside the step, after the permit is taken), through the real `Node::shutdown`: it begins while the step is held; the close does not finish while it is held; Raft is still running; release → the step reaches its terminal return, the close finishes, `CLEAN_SHUTDOWN` is recorded, and after a restart the store is not in recovery and reads are exact (the step's outcome asserted precisely: the published postimage, or a refusal whose durable intent is still recoverable). Fails on today's code (the abort revokes the record).
2. **Past the bound, fail closed:** a hold never released (the bound shortened for the test) → the service is aborted and joined, `CLEAN_SHUTDOWN` is never written, the next start is unclean.
3. **A stopped service admits no new tick:** once the service has observed the stop (including while paused or sleeping), no new tick is admitted (a counter of tick admissions); a tick admitted before the stop may finish.
4. **The census check** (Decision 5) passes, and fails for an unclassified permit site.
5. **The workflow:** `certified_tier_cleanup_rewrites_and_retires_the_leaders_tiered_slabs` ≥5 runs under load at the final tip, the rate reported; then the consolidated checkpoint.

## Not established
- A general shutdown redesign: no other service changes.
- The drain bound's adequacy for a slow object store: past it, the close is unclean (availability), as today.
- DC-196, §8, and ADR 0705's open items (unchanged).
