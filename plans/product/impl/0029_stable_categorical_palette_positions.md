# Stable categorical palette positions

Scope: implement PRODUCT-0029 only. Deliverable is one combined ADR/plan commit,
one pre-refactor compatibility-oracle commit, one position/continuation commit,
one explicit palette-resolution commit, one exact band-fill rendering commit,
one boundary/regression commit, and one evidence commit. PRODUCT-0030 remains the
first Rune palette surface and production caller of the explicit path.

## Halt rule

Stop on any libaryon theme, palette, name, modifier, or natural-length change;
Rune grammar/envelope; execution, transport, panels, placement, dashboard,
tables, or workspace configuration; an implicit color-channel binding;
renderer-side palette parsing, position assignment, or categorical sampling;
moving exact colors out of `ResolvedPlotSpec`; changing a non-categorical
`ColorJob`; silently retargeting a continuation between allocation spaces; or
migrating every `ColorTheme` consumer merely to supply this selection.

## Stage 0: feasibility findings

### 0a. One position representation can preserve the legacy entrypoint

`resolve_domains` is the production entrypoint used by plot requests and durable
plots. Its categorical branch is isolated in `resolve_category_colors`, with a
twelve-color constant and sequential ordinal assignment. Replace the retained
RGB with a validated `PalettePosition`, but keep this entrypoint's twelve-slot
allocation space, sequential assignment, exact colors, errors, and lack of band
fills. It remains correct until PRODUCT-0030 switches the production caller
atomically with an authored selection.

Add a second explicit entrypoint for PRODUCT-0028 palettes. Both may share
allocation machinery, but each lineage records its allocation-space kind and
length. Mixing legacy and explicit spaces, or explicit spaces of different
natural lengths, refuses without mutation. No shared helper owns a default.

### 0b. Bank old visible behavior before moving continuation state

Before changing production source, add a checked-in textual oracle generated by
ordinary tests through the current public resolver and bar factory. One line per
case records category-to-exact-color mappings for `n=0..12`, filtering and
reappearance, the thirteen-category refusal, and the current two-field bar's
ordered SVG fill operations.

The oracle observes resolved mappings and rendered operations, not the private
twelve-color constant. It is banked in a source-test-only commit and rerun
unchanged after the refactor. It proves compatibility, not the explicit path.

### 0c. The explicit selection owns the libaryon result

`Theme::try_new(identity)` owns its optional categorical palette, so a selection
cannot borrow through a temporary `Theme`. Use a product-side owned
`CategoricalPaletteSelection` containing the exact identity and a clone of the
immutable `ResolvedCategoricalPalette`. Its only production constructor resolves
through libaryon once and distinguishes unknown identity from known absence. It
cannot accept arbitrary colors.

### 0d. Position allocation precedes resolved colors

Resolve and validate the full domain and both capacities, allocate positions in
a cloned continuation, then project positions to exact `ResolvedColor` values.
Publish the clone only after every step succeeds. Pure even-spread and maximin
tests supplement, but do not replace, successive live-resolution assays.

### 0e. Exact band fills already have a palette-free route to bars

`ResolvedPlotSpec` reaches `factory::create_plot`, which constructs
`BarPlotKernel`. Carry resolved `BandFillAssignment` values through that seam.
The bar draw path selects an exact assignment by resolved category; when absent
it retains legacy sampling. Legend code remains unchanged because band fill is
styling, not an encoding.

No `Theme`, categorical palette, allocator, or `ColorIdentity` enters the
factory or kernel. Halt if exact fills require changing hover, geometry, axes,
guides, or another mark.

### 0f. Polariton composes capacity and selection facts

Libaryon reports name resolution, optional capability, natural length, and exact
colors. Polariton owns capability and structured capacity failures because only
it knows field, layer, role, and geometry limit. Unknown, absent, and
insufficient outcomes remain distinct.

## Stage 1: bank the compatibility oracle

Add the Stage 0b resolver and rendered-bar snapshot with no production-source
change. Keep complete mappings inline so a diff names the affected category and
color. This stage discharges no gate; Stage 5 consumes its pre-refactor evidence.

Commit: `test: bank legacy categorical plot oracle`.

## Stage 2: retain validated positions without changing legacy behavior

Add private immutable `PalettePosition` construction checked against an
allocation-space length. Replace `ColorIdentity`'s baked colors with positions
and record each lineage's closed allocation-space descriptor. Preserve the
unselected resolver through the legacy twelve-slot space: sequential assignment,
exact colors, PRODUCT-0007 lifecycle, and no band fills.

Completed specifications remain self-contained by projecting positions to exact
colors. Update PRODUCT-0007 tests to observe positions for continuation and
colors for artifacts. Rerun Stage 1 unchanged.

PRODUCT-0029 gates: 1, 2, 13 (representation and compatibility half).

Commit: `impl: retain categorical palette positions`.

## Stage 3: resolve explicit selections and stable growth

Add `CategoricalPaletteSelection::from_theme_name` and an explicit resolver.
Implement fresh even spread, live maximin extension, filtered reservation, and
projection through PRODUCT-0028's exact sequence. Compare complete category to
position to color mappings.

Add structured product-side failures for absent capability, capacity, and an
incompatible continuation space. Capacity validation computes final cardinality
before mutating a clone and reports geometry-only, palette-only, and both-limit
facts together.

Assays cover exact `tab20c`, `_r`, and `_s1`; `n=0`, `n=1`, all admitted `n`,
and the greedy counterexample; three returned live identities producing
`A->0`, `B->19`, `C->9` plus a fresh `0,10,19` control; filtering and changed
fields; all capacity cases with follow-up success; and unknown, `nord`, and
`dark2` as distinct outcomes. Direct selector calls alone cannot prove the live
gate.

PRODUCT-0029 gates: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

Commit: `impl: resolve stable categorical palettes`.

## Stage 4: carry and render exact categorical band fills

Add `BandFillAssignment` to `ResolvedPlotSpec`. In the explicit path only, a bar
with absent `Channel::Color` allocates over its categorical band domain and
carries one exact fill per category. A bar with authored categorical color
carries channel assignments and no band fills.

Pass exact fills through the factory into `BarPlotKernel`. Draw them when
present; otherwise retain `ColorTheme` sampling byte-for-byte. Do not alter
legend construction or consult palette state in the kernel.

Use an SVG/recording assay for six exact fills in category draw order plus a
structural absent-Color assertion. Add authored-channel, scatter, line, and
legacy controls. Inspecting only a stored vector does not discharge gate 10.

PRODUCT-0029 gates: 10, 11, 12.

Commit: `impl: render resolved categorical bar fills`.

## Stage 5: guard boundaries and close regressions

Rerun Stage 1, all PRODUCT-0007 lineage/capacity/pinning tests, and the complete
suite unchanged in behavioral expectation. Add architecture coverage that no
libaryon source changed; Polariton does not parse modifiers; only spec resolution
mentions `Theme` or the resolved categorical palette; kernels receive exact
colors only; Rune and production requests select no explicit palette; the
unselected resolver remains visibly compatibility-only; and band fill cannot
originate `Channel::Color` or enter non-bar marks.

Source scans are reintroduction guards, not behavioral proof.

PRODUCT-0029 gates: 13 (full regression half), 14.

Commit: `test: guard categorical palette position boundaries`.
